How many customer groups is right?

There's no single "correct" number of groups — too few and different shoppers get lumped together, too many and the groups start to overlap and lose meaning. These three views each measure grouping quality at different numbers of groups. The dashed line marks the number we recommend for your customer list, where all three broadly agree.
